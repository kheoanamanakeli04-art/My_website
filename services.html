Adventure and Outdoor Activities

Lesotho is an outdoor arena like no other. Ditch the ordinary and challenge yourself with adventures that you literally cannot find anywhere else on the planet.

Conquering Maletsunyane Falls:

Strap in for the ultimate rush and conquer the world's highest commercial single-drop abseil, descending a jaw-dropping 204 meters alongside a roaring waterfall.




Pony Trekking and Horse Riding

Explore the rugged Maluti Mountains, visit remote valleys or ride down to the base of waterfalls on traditional Basotho ponies.




Hiking and Mountain Biking

Trek across scenic paths, high-altitude passes like the Mafika Lisiu and national parks like Ts'ehlanyane National Park.





Skiing & Snowboarding

Visit Afriski Mountain Resort during winter months(May to July) for skiing snowboarding .

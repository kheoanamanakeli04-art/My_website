Experience the Warmth of the Hihglands

Khotso! Welcome to Lesotho, where our legendary mounatin hospitality is as high as our peaks. Our Kingdom invites you to experience a world of unrivaled natural beauty-from the thunderous roar of majestic waterfalls and prastine, snow-dusted peaks to the engineering marvels of our sweeping blue dams.

Whether you are seeking the thrill of a 4*4 alpine pass, tranquility of a pony trek through remote villages, or absolute standing freedom of standing atop our endless basalt cliffs, the Kingdom in the Sky promises adventure that stays with you forever.

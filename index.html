Welcome to Lesotho

Lesotho is a beautiful mountain kingdom, entirely above 1 400m. Explore waterfalls, dams, national parks and Basotho culture.


Why Visit?

  • Stunning mountain scenery - Kingdom in the Sky
  • Rich Basotho culture, blankets and music
  • Friendly people and warm hospitality
  • Adventure - hiking, pony trekking and skiing

What To Do?

  • Visit Sehlabathebe National Park
  • See Maletsunyane Falls
  • Tour Katse Dam
  • Go pony trekking in the mountains
